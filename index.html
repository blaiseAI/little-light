Little stories, big light

Five-minute Bible stories and games for kids aged 5 to 7. Hear the story, learn a verse by heart, and light a star in the night sky every time you finish. Twenty-one stories, from Creation and the Sabbath to the Good Samaritan.

Your night sky

Finish a story to light its star. Each week has seven stars, one for each day of creation week.

Build the verse, word by word

Kids tap the words into order and meet the story's verse again and again, until it's theirs to keep.

A little light, every day

One short story a day keeps the sun up. Big buttons, pictures before words, and when an answer isn't right yet, it simply comes back later. Nobody fails here.

"Let your light shine before others."Matthew 5:16

Made for Sabbath-keeping families and Sabbath School, and for any child who loves Bible stories. Little Light is a design study.
